# Tarot card images

Rider–Waite–Smith tarot, illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith, published 1909 by Rider & Company.
Public domain (copyright expired).

Scans of an original 1909 "Pam-A" deck, cleaned up and restored by Steve P. (https://steve-p.org/cards/RWSa.html),
obtained via https://github.com/mixvlad/TarotCards (tarot/rider-waite/720px), resized to 320 px WebP.

Thai meanings (upright and reversed) were written for this project.
